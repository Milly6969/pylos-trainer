How to play

Goal: place the final sphere at the pyramid's peak. Each side starts with 15 spheres.

On your turn: place one sphere into any empty, supported hollow; or lift one of your uncovered spheres to a higher supported level.

Make a square: completing a 2×2 square of your own colour lets you reclaim one or two of your uncovered spheres, returning them to your reserve. In this trainer, reclaiming is optional.

Advanced: completing a straight row/column of four on level 1 or three on level 2 also triggers reclaiming.

When your reserve runs out and you cannot lift, you lose. The four boards show the stacked levels from base to peak. Highlighted hollows are legal moves.
